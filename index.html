Wire It Up! — Privacy Policy

Developer: Ami Works · Updated: October 22, 2026

This policy covers the Android edition of Wire It Up! (package com.amiworks.wireitup), including its AdMob-supported version. Versions distributed on other platforms may be subject to that platform's additional policies.

Data and local storage

All levels and game assets are bundled in the app and can be played offline. Progress and settings are not sent to our own server. There are no accounts, in-app purchases, Firebase Analytics, or CrazyGames SDK in this Android edition. This app includes Google AdMob advertising; advertising data processing is described below.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device/account identifiers such as the advertising ID, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google and its advertising partners use these data for advertising, measurement, personalization where permitted, security and fraud prevention. Ad delivery requires Internet access. If an ad is unavailable, you can continue playing.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform to request and apply privacy choices where required before requesting ads. Where required, an “Ad privacy choices” button in the level menu lets you revisit those choices. Declining personalized advertising does not block game progress; limited or non-personalized ads may still process data. Google manages advertising-data retention and rights under its policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies. You can also manage or reset the advertising ID in Android settings.

Completed levels, unlocked levels, best star scores, language, tutorial preferences, and sound settings are stored locally in the app's private WebView storage. They remain on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android cloud backup is disabled by this app. Progress is not automatically shared with the browser edition, CrazyGames, or other devices.

Permissions and services

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, notification, or shared-file access. Internet and network-state access support the runtime and advertisements. The advertising SDK may declare advertising-ID and Android ad-services permissions. The app does not request a GPS/location runtime permission. Google Play and Android may process installation, diagnostics, or other data independently under their own settings and policies.

Contact and changes

If you choose to email support, we receive the information you include and use it to answer your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information. For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, contact amiworks.dev@gmail.com. We retain support correspondence only as needed to address your request or meet applicable obligations. Updates to data handling will be reflected in this policy before the corresponding app release.

資料與本機儲存

關卡與素材隨 App 安裝，可離線遊玩。進度與設定不會傳送到我們自己的伺服器。本 Android 版沒有帳號、內購、Firebase Analytics 或 CrazyGames SDK；含有 Google AdMob 廣告，其資料處理方式如下。

廣告與隱私選擇

Google Mobile Ads（AdMob）可能收集與分享 IP 位址（可推估概略位置）、裝置／帳戶識別碼（例如廣告識別碼）、廣告互動與診斷資訊。Google 與廣告合作夥伴會用於廣告、成效衡量、在允許時提供個人化內容、安全及防詐。廣告需要連網；沒有可用廣告時仍可繼續遊戲。

我們使用 Google User Messaging Platform，在有要求時先取得及套用隱私選擇再請求廣告。在需要的地區，可從關卡選單的「廣告隱私選擇」重新管理。拒絕個人化廣告不會阻擋進度；有限或非個人化廣告仍可能處理資料。Google 依其政策管理廣告資料的保存及相關權利。請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及 Google 廣告技術，也可在 Android 設定管理或重設廣告識別碼。

已完成及解鎖關卡、最高星數、語言、教學提示與音效設定，儲存在 App 私有的 WebView 本機空間，直到你清除 App 資料或解除安裝。App 已停用 Android 雲端備份。進度不會自動與瀏覽器版、CrazyGames 或其他裝置同步。

權限與平台服務

App 不要求相機、麥克風、通訊錄、精確定位、通知或共用檔案權限。網際網路及網路狀態權限支援執行環境與廣告。廣告 SDK 可能宣告廣告識別碼及 Android 廣告服務權限；App 不要求 GPS／定位執行階段權限。Google Play 與 Android 可能依各自設定及政策處理安裝、診斷等資料。
