Wire It Up! — Privacy Policy

Developer: Ami Works · Updated: October 22, 2026

This policy covers the offline Android edition of Wire It Up! (package com.amiworks.wireitup). Versions distributed on other platforms may be subject to that platform's additional policies.

Data and local storage

The Android game does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, location, gameplay analytics, or advertising data to the developer or third parties. There are no accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics services, or CrazyGames SDK in this edition. All levels and game assets are bundled in the app and can be played offline.

Completed levels, unlocked levels, best star scores, language, tutorial preferences, and sound settings are stored locally in the app's private WebView storage. They remain on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android cloud backup is disabled by this app. Progress is not automatically shared with the browser edition, CrazyGames, or other devices.

Permissions and services

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, notification, or shared-file access. Android's Internet permission is included for the WebView runtime; the game itself uses bundled local assets and has no remote game service. Google Play and Android may process installation, diagnostics, or other data independently under their own settings and policies.

Contact and changes

If you choose to email support, we receive the information you include and use it to answer your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information. For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, contact amiworks.dev@gmail.com. We retain support correspondence only as needed to address your request or meet applicable obligations. Updates to data handling will be reflected in this policy before the corresponding app release.

資料與本機儲存

Android 遊戲不會向開發者或第三方收集或傳送個人資訊、裝置識別碼、位置、遊玩分析或廣告資料。本版沒有帳號、廣告、內購、分析服務或 CrazyGames SDK。關卡與素材隨 App 安裝，可離線遊玩。

已完成及解鎖關卡、最高星數、語言、教學提示與音效設定，儲存在 App 私有的 WebView 本機空間，直到你清除 App 資料或解除安裝。App 已停用 Android 雲端備份。進度不會自動與瀏覽器版、CrazyGames 或其他裝置同步。

權限與平台服務

App 不要求相機、麥克風、通訊錄、定位、通知或共用檔案權限。Android 網路權限用於 WebView 執行環境；遊戲使用內建素材，沒有遠端遊戲服務。Google Play 與 Android 可能依各自設定及政策處理安裝、診斷等資料。
